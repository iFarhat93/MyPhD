How to use this page

Twenty-one steps walk from the question the thesis asked to the silicon it produced. Each step has the key points, the thesis figures, an expert note with the exact numbers, and a narrated transcript. Press play on any step, or play the whole story and let it scroll with the voice.

Guided mode keeps the expert notes folded; Expert mode opens them all.
Keyboard: space plays or pauses, ← and → change step. Click any figure to enlarge it.
New to CPUs, GPUs, FPGAs and ASICs? Read the companion page The Silicon Ladder first: the four platforms level by level, with a quiz. For the short version of the thesis, see the story in 10 scenes.
